How to use this calculator

Enter your target, current savings and months remaining.

How it's calculated

Monthly saving = (goal − already saved) ÷ months remaining.

Worked example

Goal 5,000,000, already saved 500,000, 12 months left: 4,500,000 ÷ 12 = 375,000 a month.
