Quick guide

  • Enter the base and the exponent.
  • Negative, decimal and fraction exponents work.
  • Whole-number powers are exact.

How to use this calculator

Enter a base and an exponent. For whole-number exponents the answer is exact. For fraction or decimal exponents you get a decimal answer.

How it's calculated

aⁿ = a × a × … (n times). a⁻ⁿ = 1 ÷ aⁿ. a^(p/q) is the q-th root of aᵖ. Any non-zero number to the power 0 is 1.

Worked example

2 to the power 10 = 1,024. 2 to the power −3 = 1/8. 9 to the power 1/2 = 3.

Frequently asked questions

What does a negative exponent mean?

It means take the reciprocal: 2⁻³ = 1 ÷ 2³ = 1/8.

What is a fractional exponent?

The power 1/2 is a square root, 1/3 is a cube root, and so on.
