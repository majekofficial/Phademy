How it's calculated

Momentum p = mv. Impulse: Ft = m(v − u). In a collision with no outside forces, total momentum stays the same: m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂.

Worked example

A 4 kg ball moving at 6 m/s has momentum 4 × 6 = 24 kg m/s.

Frequently asked questions

What is impulse?

Force multiplied by the time it acts. It equals the change in momentum.

How do I show direction?

Use positive for one direction and negative for the opposite one.
